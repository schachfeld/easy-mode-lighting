# antislop audit 001 (2026-11-06)

Scope: the React Native app in `native/src/` and the Home Assistant add-on UI in `src/`.
Mode: after (audit only, nothing changed).
Method: I read the source and computed WCAG contrast ratios for the theme tokens in `native/src/theme.tsx` and `src/styles.css`. Both apps use the same two palettes. I did not run either app for this audit, so nothing here comes from a device or a browser.

Overall: the UI is not slop. It has a real identity: the firefly-jar palette, DM Sans, the `glow.` wordmark with its accent dot, and the scene orbs. It has no fake numbers, testimonials, dead links or em dashes, and it has empty, loading and error states. Most problems are in the **Daylight** theme, which misses WCAG AA almost everywhere, and in the add-on's touch targets on phones.

## HIGH (Hard Gate)

1. **R-25: Daylight secondary text fails AA in both apps.** `muted` #8d9082 is 3.04:1 on `bg` and 3.18:1 on `surface`. It is used at 12 to 14px for every card subtitle ("3 of 5 lights on", "Off", "2700 K · Warm white"), empty-state body text and form hints. `textSecondary` #72766c is 4.33:1 on `bg`, just under the limit, and the add-on shows it at 11px. Firefly passes (6.4:1 and 9.5:1).
2. **R-25: Daylight primary and danger buttons fail AA.** `onAccent` on `accent` is 2.91:1 ("Sign in with Home Assistant", "Open my home", the primary buttons in the editors). `dangerOn` on `dangerFill` is 3.42:1. Firefly passes (6.19:1 and 4.84:1).
3. **R-25: Daylight status and notice colors fail AA.** `okInk` on `okBg` is 3.09:1 (the "Connected" pill and the 10px "Home Assistant" scene badge), `warnInk` on `warnBg` is 2.65:1 (the "Connecting…" pill), `warnInkStrong` on `warnBg` is 3.45:1 (warning notices), and `dangerInk` on `dangerBg` is 3.79:1 (error notices and error toasts). `leaf` is 3.21:1 on `bg` (the 11px connect-screen line and the Settings icons).
4. **R-25: `faint` is used as text.** The version line in Settings (`SettingsScreen.tsx:137`) is 4.14:1 in Firefly and 2.22:1 in Daylight at 12px. Input placeholders use `faint` too (2.22:1 in Daylight). On the connect screen the placeholder `http://homeassistant.local:8123` is the only hint of what format the address takes, so it needs to be readable.
5. **R-25 / R-39: Daylight switches and sliders are hard to see.** WCAG asks for 3:1 for control boundaries. The off track `toggleOff` is 1.71:1 and its border 1.88:1 against `surface`. The on color `toggleOn` is 2.77:1, and the brightness fill `sliderFill` is 1.91:1, so the brightness level is barely visible. These are the main controls of the app. Firefly's off track is 1.66:1, but its border (3.34:1) carries the shape.
6. **R-32: In the add-on, the Daylight focus ring is too faint.** `button:focus-visible` and `a:focus-visible` use `--accent` #df7855, which is 2.82:1 on the Daylight `bg`. Inputs use `--focus` #df785588, which is translucent and fainter still (`src/styles.css:181-188`).
7. **R-03: In the add-on, touch targets on phones are under 44px.** These are: `.icon-button` 30×30, `.toggle` 35×20, `.compact-scene .scene-favorite` 23×23, `.button` 37px tall and `.all-off` 33px tall. The `max-width: 700px` block shrinks the buttons further (10px text, 9px padding) and never enlarges a target. The native app is fine, because `hitSlop` brings its controls to 44px or more.

## MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)

8. **R-04: Sparkles is the icon for Scenes.** It appears in the native tab bar (`app/(home)/_layout.tsx:93`), the Scenes empty state, the Settings "How it works" list, the add-on navigation (`App.tsx:308`), `ConnectionHelp.tsx:117` and `ScenesPage.tsx:66`. It is the stock "AI magic" glyph, and Glow already has its own mark for a scene: the `SceneOrb`. A small orb, or a palette glyph, would tie the tab to what a scene looks like in the app.
9. **R-04: All icons come from Lucide, and no reason is written down.** That covers the tab bar, the room icons, the bulbs and the buttons. The glyphs are relevant, so this is mostly a documentation gap. Either write the reason down (for example "Lucide 1.5 to 1.8 stroke to match the DM Sans weight"), or draw the few icons that carry the brand (tabs, empty states) yourself.
10. **R-06 / R-09: The connect screen has an eyebrow above the headline.** "YOUR HOME, IN A GOOD LIGHT" is 11px uppercase with 1.4 letter-spacing, in `leaf` (`ConnectScreen.tsx:70-77`), right above "A little closer to home." This is the eyebrow pattern, and in Daylight it also fails contrast (#3). It is a real tagline (the add-on uses it in its `<title>`), so it could go into the subheading or under the wordmark instead.
11. **R-06: Every section label is uppercase with letter-spacing.** `FieldLabel` (`ui.tsx:290-302`) sets 12px uppercase with 0.6 spacing for "Appearance", "How it works" and the form labels. The spacing is mild, but the pattern needs a written reason or a sentence-case label.

## LOW (Quality Locks)

12. **R-15: An empty state has the button "Explore scenes".** In the Favorites empty state (`ScenesScreen.tsx:65`, `ScenesPage.tsx:79`) the button only clears the filter. "Show all scenes" says what it does and matches "Show all rooms" on the Rooms screen.
13. **R-15: An empty state has the button "How it works".** The no-rooms empty state (`RoomsScreen.tsx:143`) uses it, and the button navigates to Settings. A label that names the destination, such as "Open Settings & help", would tell people where it goes.
14. **C-3: Light cards have a redundant link.** "Adjust light" / "Adjust group" (`cards.tsx:192-201`) does the same thing as tapping the light's name and icon right above it. It adds a row to every card. Either keep it and drop the row's tap action, or drop the link.
15. **R-31 / R-37: Glow has no `DESIGN.md`.** The design direction exists, but only as code comments (`theme.tsx`, the header of `styles.css`) and the logo explorations in `design/`. A short `DESIGN.md` would keep the two apps from drifting apart. It would record the firefly-jar palette, the reason for DM Sans, the radius scale (12 / 16), the dials (I read them as ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1) and the contrast floor for both themes.

## Passed, with evidence

- R-02: no em dash in UI text in either app (grep of `native/src` and `src`).
- R-17 / R-18 / R-36 / R-38: there are no statistics, testimonials or trust claims. The one number on Rooms ("N lights on across M rooms") is live data.
- R-24 / R-26: the only external link (Home Assistant "organizing" docs) works, and the empty-state buttons go to real screens.
- R-27: there is a loading screen, empty states for rooms, filters and scenes, an error notice and an error toast, and a lost-connection banner (`Overlays.tsx:249`).
- R-39: native controls set `accessibilityRole`, a label and a state. The add-on sets `cursor: pointer` and `not-allowed`, `lang`, and a `<title>`, does not block zoom, and honors `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- R-10 / R-12 / R-13: blur is used in 2 places in the add-on, and there is one halo glow, behind the connect-screen bulb.
- R-20: the design does not look generic with the logo swapped out, because of the palette, the orbs and the per-room tones.
