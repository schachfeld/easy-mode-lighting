# antislop audit 001: fixes (2026-11-06)

You approved all 15 findings from `audit-001-2026-11-06.md`. All of them are fixed.

## What changed

1. to 5. **Daylight contrast (R-25).** I darkened the Daylight tokens without changing their hues, in `native/src/theme.tsx` and `src/styles.css` (the two stay in step). Every text token now reaches at least 4.5:1 on `bg`, `surface`, `surfaceSunken` and `hover`. Every control color reaches at least 3:1 on `surface`. Text on the accent now uses the dark `onAccent` ink, as Firefly already did. In Firefly I only changed `faint`, from 4.14:1 to 4.62:1. I checked all 52 token pairs against the stylesheet: none fails.
   - One more text use of a room tint turned up on the device: the native room header subtitle (`RoomScreen.tsx:86`, 3.15:1). It now uses `textSecondary` (5.82:1 or more on every room background).
   - In the add-on, the white-preset names were 10px in the tint color. They are now 12px in `--text-secondary`, and the icon keeps the tint.
6. **Add-on focus ring (R-32).** `--focus` is now a solid color in both themes (5.59:1 in Firefly, 5.40:1 in Daylight). Buttons, links, `summary`, inputs and the color wheel all use it.
7. **Add-on touch targets (R-03).** `.icon-button` and `.button` now get an invisible 44px `:after` touch area, as the switch already had. **Correction to the audit:** `.toggle` already had a 44×44 touch area, so that part of the finding was wrong. The phone layout no longer shrinks button text to 10px and 9px.
8. **Sparkles icon (R-04).** It is replaced by a palette icon in all six places: Lucide `Palette`, and the SF Symbol `paintpalette` in the iOS tab bar. Scenes in Glow are color palettes, so the icon describes the content.
9. **Lucide reason (R-04).** Written down in `DESIGN.md` under Icons.
10. **Eyebrow (R-06 / R-09).** The uppercase line above the connect-screen headline is gone. "Your home, in a good light" now sits under the wordmark in sentence case.
11. **Uppercase labels (R-06).** `FieldLabel` is now 14px semibold in sentence case, with no letter-spacing.
12. **"Explore scenes"** is now "Show all scenes" in both apps.
13. **"How it works"** is now "Open Settings & help" in both apps.
14. **"Adjust light" link (C-3).** Removed from light cards in both apps. The light's row (bulb icon and name) is the control. It is labelled "Adjust <light name>" and shows a chevron, as room rows do. Group cards keep their "Individual lights" toggle. I updated the browser and Home Assistant tests to use the row.
15. **`DESIGN.md` (R-31 / R-37).** Added at the repo root, with the dials, palette, contrast floor, type, radii, icons and copy rules. `CLAUDE.md` points to it.
